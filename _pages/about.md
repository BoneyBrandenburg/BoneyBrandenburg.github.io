I am a Ph.D. in Computer Science at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Daqing Zhang](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~daqingz/) (IEEE Fellow). My research bridges the physical and digital worlds by turning ambient wireless signals into robust sensing channels for human activity understanding.

## About Me

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computer Science, Peking University. My work focuses on practical sensing methods with commercial WiFi devices and has been published at venues such as **ACM UbiComp**, **IMWUT**, and **ACM SenSys**.
